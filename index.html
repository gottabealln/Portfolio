Booking.com Web Scraper

A Python automation tool built with Selenium that scrapes hotel listings from Booking.com based on the user's chosen destination, dates, and preferences. It collects key details such as hotel names, prices, ratings, and availability, then organizes them into a clean, structured format for easy comparison. Ideal for travellers and analysts who want fast, data-driven decisions without manually browsing hundreds of listings.
